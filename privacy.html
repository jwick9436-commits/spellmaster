📋 Privacy Policy

Last updated:

At SpellMaster, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide you with the best learning experience:

  • Learning Progress: Your spelling progress, scores, and words learned are stored in your browser's local storage.
  • Account Data: If you create an account, your username and learning progress are stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To track your learning progress and display performance metrics
  • To save your preferences and settings
  • To improve your learning experience

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using localStorage. We do NOT store any data on external servers. Your information stays on your device.

4. Cookies

We do not use cookies for tracking. However, if you enable Google AdSense ads, they may place cookies for personalized advertising. You can manage these settings through your browser.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense: Displays ads. They may collect data as per their Privacy Policy.
  • Web Speech API: Used for text-to-speech functionality. No data is sent to external servers.

6. Your Rights

  • You can clear all your data at any time by clearing your browser's cache and localStorage.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data from the Profile section.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Contact Us
